# Write-off, undo, and hitch

A Flip leaves Inventory by a standing Sale, a Re-split (Retired), or a standing Write-off — donated, trashed, or lost; one act, one or more Flips. Keep-for-self is not an exit. When the Flip is in the Operator's hands again, that Sale or Write-off is Undone, per Flip. Leftover Marketplace fee, Outbound shipping (including return postage), and Supplies hitch onto the Flip and enter Profit on the next standing Sale or Write-off, including via a Retired ancestor. Listing spend freezes once any Flip on that Listing has a standing Sale or Write-off. A live Listing blocks Write-off.

## Considered options

- **Keep-for-self leaves Inventory** — still owned, might still sell; listing it later would need an undo. Tag it instead.
- **$0 Sale for donate/trash/lost** — Sale means sold. Write-off is the second realizing event, $0 Proceeds, same cost lines.
- **Costs vanish on undo** — a return looks free; Outbound shipping already paid would disappear.
- **Book a loss on undo while Inventory** — one Flip, two P&L moments; Inventory would no longer be Acquisition cost only.
- **Whole kit or bag undoes** — the buyer kept two shirts, or Goodwill took four; those events stood.
- **Return postage as Inbound shipping** — would rewrite Acquisition cost.
- **Write-off on a live Listing** — would silently end a Listing to trash a Flip. End the Listing first, as with Re-split.

## Consequences

- A Flip has at most one standing Sale or one standing Write-off, never both, and may have many Undone Sales and Undone Write-offs.
- Profit exists without a Sale when a Write-off stands; it happens on Write-off date. Same formula.
- Inventory stays Acquisition cost. Hitch is not a new cost name. Dump / hauler / ship-to-charity amounts are Outbound shipping on the Write-off.
- Tag Profit includes written-off Flips; Inventory by Tag excludes them; counts split unsold vs sold vs written-off.
- Partial refunds where the buyer keeps the Flip are not undo.
